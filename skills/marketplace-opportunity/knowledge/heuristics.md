# Practitioner heuristics

Useful rules of thumb, but they are drawn mostly from marketplaces that survived. Treat them as hypotheses to check, not laws.

| Heuristic | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Fragmentation on both sides; control of payment flow ("if you bring the cash, you are king") | Bill Gurley, "All Markets Are Not Created Equal" (2012), [link](https://abovethecrowd.com/2012/11/13/all-markets-are-not-created-equal-10-factors-to-consider-when-evaluating-digital-marketplaces/) | Structure and payment-control criteria |
| "The most dangerous strategy for any platform company is to price too high." A low base rake plus optional paid visibility | Gurley, "A Rake Too Far" (2013), [link](https://abovethecrowd.com/2013/04/18/a-rake-too-far-optimal-platformpricing-strategy/) | Pricing strategy. Note that "optional" ads have become near-compulsory (Etsy Offsite Ads are mandatory above $10k a year), which regulators may read as hidden fees. |
| Constrain the launch by geography or category; most early effort goes into supply | Lenny Rachitsky, 17 marketplaces: nearly all constrained, 14 supply-first, [link](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-kickstart-and-scale-a-marketplace) | Atomic network; hard-side acquisition |
| Atomic network (the smallest network that works on its own); the hard side | Andrew Chen, *The Cold Start Problem* (2021) | Liquidity plan |
| Aggregators win by owning the user relationship and commoditising suppliers | Ben Thompson, "Aggregation Theory" (2015), [link](https://stratechery.com/2015/aggregation-theory/) | Discovery dependency; incumbent proximity |
| "Own the work, not just the transaction" | Speedinvest (Sep 2026), [link](https://www.speedinvest.com/knowledge/own-the-work-not-just-the-transaction-why-were-still-bullish-on-marketplaces). Investor essay with portfolio interest | Strategy in the AI era |
| AI revives "graveyard" managed categories by cutting coordination cost | Olivia Moore, a16z (Nov 2025), [link](https://a16z.com/marketplaces-in-the-age-of-ai-take-two-graveyard-to-greenfield/). Investor essay; no outcome data yet | Generation operator `ai_revive_graveyard`; treat as hypothesis |
| Agents will make the marketplace disappear, versus marketplaces become the "trust API" agents need | Casey Winters (2025), [link](https://www.caseyaccidental.com/p/when-agents-attack-how-ai-collapses), against b2venture (2026), [link](https://b2venture.vc/stories/part-i---marketplaces-x-ai-the-end-of-marketplaces-as-we-know-them). Experts disagree | Agent-bypass criterion; red-team scenario |

## Heuristics to distrust

- NFX's claim that network effects created "70% of all the value in technology since 1994" comes without data (low confidence). Do not cite it.
- Any "first-mover" story. The history of marketplaces is full of second movers that won on governance and trust.
- Vendor market sizes, such as resale reports sponsored by resale operators. They are often survey-based and internally inconsistent (low confidence).
