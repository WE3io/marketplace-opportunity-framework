# Context as of November 2026

Full sourcing is in `docs/research/state-of-marketplaces-2026.md`. **After the valid_until date, treat every figure here as stale and re-verify it.**

## Where profit comes from

- **Above the commission line.** Large marketplaces earn their margin from advertising, fulfilment and financial services. Amazon's Q3 2026 advertising revenue ($19.6bn) was more than twice its North American operating income ($8.7bn) (company-reported; high). Etsy's take rate rose to 26.2% in Q3 2026 because of ads, not fees. Nearly half of Walmart's US marketplace volume runs through Walmart Fulfillment Services.
- **Retail media is concentrating.** WARC credits Amazon with 78% of US retail media in 2025 and forecasts 2027 growth outside Amazon of only 9.8% (analyst; moderate-high). Sub-scale marketplaces should not plan on large ad revenue.
- **Sellers' all-in costs on Amazon are about 39% to over 50%** of revenue once fees and ads are included (independent analysis and vendor model; moderate).

## Where growth is

- **Fast:** MercadoLibre (GMV +42% in Q1 2026), Shopee (+28% in Q2 2026), TikTok Shop (H1 2026 GMV ~$50bn, +92%; a single-source analyst estimate) and recommerce (Vinted 2025 GMV €10.8bn, +47%).
- **Slow:** the mature Western horizontals grow in low-to-mid single digits organically.
- **UK:** online was 28.8% of retail in September 2026 (ONS). Vinted holds 50-60% of UK consumer-to-consumer fashion GMV (CMA, as relayed). Temu reached 28.4m UK adults in May 2025 (Ofcom).

## AI and agents

- **Discovery has shifted more than checkout.** AI-referred visitors to US retail sites converted 60% better than other traffic in July 2026 (Adobe; measured).
- **In-chat checkout stumbled.** OpenAI handed checkout back to merchants in March 2026. Walmart reported purchases inside ChatGPT converting at a third of the rate of click-outs (one retailer; company-reported).
- **Incumbents block outside agents.** Amazon blocks ChatGPT and Meta's agent, and eBay has reportedly banned autonomous agents. The Ninth Circuit (Aug 2026, Amazon v. Perplexity) treated a shopping agent as the user's tool, on the current record.
- **Commoditised services marketplaces are being hollowed out.** Fiverr's active buyers fell 26% year on year (Q3 2026), Upwork's gross services volume fell 10%, and Angi's revenue fell 9%.
- **The share of purchases agents complete is not known.**

## Trade and regulation

- **Customs exemptions are closing.** US de minimis was suspended globally from 29 Aug 2025. An EU €3 duty on parcels under €150 applies from 1 Jul 2026, with a handling fee from about Nov 2026. The UK's £135 relief ends "by March 2029 at the latest" (another report says Oct 2028; unresolved). Chinese cross-border platforms are moving to local warehousing.
- **The EU uses the DSA for product safety.** It fined AliExpress €550m (Jul 2026) and Temu €200m (May 2026), and opened proceedings against Shein (Feb 2026).
- **The UK is behind on marketplace duties.** Marketplace product-safety duties under the Product Regulation and Metrology Act were at consultation (Mar-Jun 2026). The CMA has direct fining powers (since Apr 2025) and opened fake-review investigations (Mar 2026). HMRC platform reporting has been live since Jan 2024.
- **Worker status.** The EU Platform Work Directive transposition deadline is 2 Dec 2026, and most member states are late. The UK is consulting on a presumption of worker status (closes 8 Jan 2027).
- **US.** Fulfilment, not listing, creates liability: the CPSC treats Amazon as a "distributor", and *Planet Green* (9th Cir. 2025) held that Section 230 does not cover physical distribution. The FTC v. Amazon trial is set for 29 Mar 2027.
- **China** restrains platforms from forcing prices *down* (platform pricing rules, effective 10 Apr 2026).

## Capital and exits

- **Funding:** venture funding for marketplaces is down about 80% from its 2021 peak (investor reading of Dealroom and Crunchbase data; moderate).
- **Exits come from consolidation and secondaries:** DoorDash–Deliveroo, Prosus–Just Eat Takeaway, eBay–Depop, Uber–Delivery Hero (agreed), DoorDash–Instacart (agreed), and Vinted's €8bn secondary.
- **Repricing is common:** Faire's 2025 tender was about 58% below its 2021 valuation.
